petwell-zern.vercel.app

PetWell

Solo project · Pet health records & vet appointments

Your pet’s health, wherever you go.

A pet health passport that belongs to the owner, not the clinic. Owners keep one record across every vet they visit; clinics read the history and add to it, and every entry stays stamped with the clinic that wrote it.

The problem

A pet's medical history lives inside whichever clinic wrote it. Change clinics and the history stays behind. Version 1 also let any clinic read every pet.

What I built

  • Separate Pet Owner and Clinic Staff roles (JWT + RBAC)
  • V2 consent rules: a clinic sees a pet only through a confirmed appointment or a one-time 6-digit share code from the owner
  • Audit log on every record a clinic opens

How I worked

Wrote the spec, ERD, endpoint list and a decision log (54 decisions) first, built section by section with AI from those documents, then checked every flow myself as both owner and staff. 88 Vitest tests run against a real database.
